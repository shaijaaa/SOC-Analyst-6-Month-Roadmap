## Study syllabus

- [ ] TCP vs UDP
- [ ] TCP three-way handshake: SYN, SYN-ACK, ACK
- [ ] Common ports: 20/21 FTP, 22 SSH, 23 Telnet, 25 SMTP, 53 DNS, 80 HTTP, 110 POP3, 143 IMAP, 443 HTTPS, 3389 RDP
- [ ] NAT, VPN, proxy, and VLAN concepts
- [ ] Open ports and network services

## Hands-on practice

- [ ] Use Nmap only against localhost, your own lab VM, or a target you have explicit permission to test.
- [ ] Use netstat and ss to inspect listening ports and network connections.
- [ ] Record scan scope, commands, results, and limitations.
